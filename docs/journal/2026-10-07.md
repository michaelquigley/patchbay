# 2026-10-07 — stage 5, quantum and monitoring

- The profiler interface needs `libpipewire-module-profiler` loaded into the client's own context (pw-top does `pw_context_load_module` for it). Without it, `pw_registry_bind` returns NULL for the Profiler and monitoring silently shows nothing. The load happens in `pb_conn_new`; a failure logs and leaves monitoring unavailable.
- Profiler pods don't wake the loop (`on_profile` calls Go once per profiler object, with no `wake`). At quantum 64 that is 750 pods a second, and waking would fold the graph that often. Summaries are recomputed on the backend tick, which is now 100ms (it was 250ms), and published only when they changed. Don't add a wake to `on_profile`.
- Live finding, decided with Michael: the work order's appearance cutoff assumes every driver's pod clock is CLOCK_MONOTONIC "now". On the desktop, gnome-shell's video driver stamps its pods with a clock about an hour behind that does not advance, so every pod was dropped and the driver vanished from monitoring.
  - The cutoff now applies per driver, only while its pod clock is within one second of CLOCK_MONOTONIC at arrival. That is re-decided on every pod and logged when it changes.
  - Other drivers' records are guarded by serial and ordering only, and say so in the inspector. The residual is accepted in `monitoring.md`. The planning agent is amending the work order's monitoring decision to match.
